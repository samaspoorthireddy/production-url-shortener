# Delegation Strategy Selection

*   **Choice**: Option B (Task-by-Task)
*   **Rationale**: For a complex feature in an unfamiliar codebase, it's safer to build and verify small pieces one at a time. This catches mistakes early, makes reviews easier, and prevents a bad assumption from affecting hundreds of lines of code.
*   **Hybrid Approach**: Use AI to generate each component quickly, but review and validate every step before moving to the next.
