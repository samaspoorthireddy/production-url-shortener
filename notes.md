# Module 2 Context Notes: Technical Writing Fundamentals

## Takeaways:
*   Knight Capital Group lost $440 million in 2012 due to a wall of dense runbook prose that delayed engineers from finding rollback instructions.
*   Technical writing is about actionability, precision, and scannability, not impressive/flowery language.
*   Like LEGO instructions, documentation should be structured, progressive, and clear enough for anyone to follow under pressure.
